1. **`compiler/driver/pipeline.ax:2255-2268` (`pipe_validate_exports` / `pipe_validate_export_item`) — False-positive `AX_LINK_DUPLICATE_EXPORT` on every `@export` method inside an `impl` block, and leaked top-level shallow-clone validation on generic `impl<T>` methods**
   - **Expected (`SPEC-26` `AXS-WLIB-021`/`AXS-WLIB-050`, `SPEC-04` `AXS-EXT-091`)**:
     1. A non-generic inherent `impl` method carrying `@export` or `@export("...")` (which `pipe_validate_exports` at `compiler/driver/pipeline.ax:2236-2242, 2258-2266` explicitly traverses `HIR_ITEM_IMPL.members` to support) must pass export validation when its exported name is unique, emit `-export:` into `.drectve`, and be exported once in `.edata` and the companion short-import `.lib`.
     2. A generic `impl<T>` method carrying `@export("...")` must report `AX_FFI_GENERIC_EXPORT` once and must not also be re-validated as a non-generic top-level function.
   - **Actual**:
     During HIR lowering (`compiler/hir/lower.ax:1605-1611`), every `HIR_ITEM_IMPL` member `m` is shallow-cloned via `clone_hir_item_shallow` (retaining identical `attrs`, `symbol`, `name`, and `span`, without copying the enclosing `impl<T>`'s `generic_params`) and appended to `ctx.hir.items`.
     `pipe_validate_exports` (`compiler/driver/pipeline.ax:2255-2268`) validates **both** `HIR_ITEM_IMPL.members` (`lines 2258-2266`) **and** every item on `ctx.hir.items` (`line 2257`), filtering out only `MonoEngine` instances via `pipe_is_mono_clone` (`line 2181`).      As a result:
     - **Case A (non-generic `impl` method with `@export`)**: Every `@export` / `@export("...")` method in an inherent `impl` block is visited twice (first in `(*it).members`, then as its shallow clone on `ctx.hir.items`) and unconditionally fails compilation with `error[AX_LINK_DUPLICATE_EXPORT]: duplicate export: <name>`.
     - **Case B (generic `impl<T>` method with `@export`)**: Visiting `m` in `(*it).members` sees `impl_is_gen == true` and emits `AX_FFI_GENERIC_EXPORT`, but visiting its shallow clone on `ctx.hir.items` passes `parent_is_generic = false` (and `(*copy).generic_params == 0`), registering the rejected generic export name in `seen_sv` and emitting a spurious `AX_LINK_DUPLICATE_EXPORT` at the exact same span whenever another item uses that export name.
