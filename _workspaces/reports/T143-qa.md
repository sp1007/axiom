### 5. Follow-ups (Confirmed from Implementation Report)

The 4 discrepancy classes between `encode.c`/`encode.ax` and GNU `as` (154 `xfail` vectors total) are accurately documented in `T143-report.md` and retained in both tables with `// xfail:` annotations:
- `AX0-FIX-ENC-LEA-SYM-REX` (8 vectors): `ax_x64_lea_sym` / `x64_lea_sym` for `r8..r15` emits `REX.B` (`0x49`) instead of `REX.R` (`0x4C`).
- `AX0-FIX-ENC-MOVX-REX` (36 vectors): `ax_x64_mov{zx,sx}_rr` / `x64_mov{zx,sx}_rr` omits mandatory `0x40` REX prefix when `src_sz == 1`, `sz in {2, 4}`, `dst < 8`, and `src in {spl, bpl, sil, dil}`.
- `AX0-FIX-ENC-ALU-RAX-IMM8` (54 vectors): `ax_x64_{add,or,and,sub,xor,cmp}_ri` with `dst == RAX`, `sz in {2, 4, 8}`, and `imm in [-128, 127]` emits the 2/4-byte accumulator immediate encoding instead of the 1-byte sign-extended `0x83 /ext ib` encoding emitted by GNU `as`.
- `AX0-FIX-ENC-CALL-REXW` (56 vectors): `ax_x64_call_{r,m}` / `x64_call_{r,m}` emits redundant `REX.W` (`0x48`) on near indirect `CALL r/m64`.
