1. **`[F1 — BLOCKER]` `spec/SPEC-84-macos-aarch64-macho.md:179` (`AXS-MACHO-025` item 2) — `CS_CodeDirectory` header omits mandatory `length: u32be` field at offset `+4` (and omits relative base of `identOffset`/`hashOffset`).**
   - **Expected:** Per Apple XNU `kern/cs_blobs.h` (`struct __CodeDirectory`, 88-byte `v0x00020400` header), offset `+0` is `magic: u32be = CS_MAGIC_CODEDIRECTORY (0xFADE0C02)` and **offset `+4` is `length: u32be = total_sig_bytes - 20`** (byte length of the `CS_CodeDirectory` blob), followed at `+8` by `version: u32be = 0x00020400`, `+12` `flags: u32be`, `+16` `hashOffset: u32be`, `+20` `identOffset: u32be`, etc. Moreover, `identOffset` (`= 88` for `v2.4.0` with `nSpecialSlots = 0` and no scatter/team ID) and `hashOffset` (`= 88 + ident_len_with_nul`) are byte offsets **relative to the start of `CS_CodeDirectory` (`sig_off + 20`)**, not `sig_off` or file offset `0`.
