### Interp twin: decision (not required, recorded follow-up)

Dropping `qa_kernel_interner.ax` from interp is acceptable for this task, but the engineer's justification is wrong and should be corrected:
- Report says "200 strings take ~27 s, 2,000 do not finish in 120 s, roughly quadratic". Measured in the snapshot with `axiomc2 run --interp` on the new test with the
  counts lowered: n=50 -> 2 s, n=200 -> 3 s, n=2,000 -> 25 s, n=10,000 -> 117 s. That is **linear** (about 11 ms per element over both tests), not quadratic;
  70,000 + 100,000 elements would be roughly 15-25 minutes in interp. Still not feasible for a gate test, so interp at the 65,536 threshold cannot be a gate test.
- Interp coverage of the interner is not lost: 49 selfhost bundles embed `compiler.kernel.interner`, and most of them run in interp (e.g. `lex/qa_lex_all`, `ast/qa_ast_all`,
  `interp/qa_interp_all`, `hir/qa_hir_all`, `drop/qa_drop_all`, `driver/qa_driver_full_interp`, ...). All of these PASSed in the gate.
- The HEAD version of the small test ran in interp in 0.5 s (and PASSed on interp/native/native-O1), so a small interp twin is cheap. It is a coverage nicety, not
  required for T544: adding it needs `tests/selfhost/kernel/gen_kernel_tests.py` and `generated-files.txt`, both outside this task's paths. Filed under Follow-ups.
