### Findings
None blocking.

Notes for the architect / follow-ups:
- **N1 (engineer's open decision, conflict extent):** the report says conflicts keep a "scope-based
  extent (until StorageDead)". In practice it is **function-wide** for named view locals, because
  pre-elaboration MIR has no `StorageDead` for them. HEAD, axiom0 and axiomc all reject:
  - `let mut b = 1; { let mv = mut_view(b); *mv = 2; } let v = view(b);` with
    `AX_CAP_CONFLICT_READ` at `view(b)`;
  - two sequential `{ let mv = mut_view(b); ... }` blocks, with `AX_CAP_CONFLICT_MUT`.

  This contradicts AXS-CAP-009 ("never past the end of its own scope"). It is not a T157 FAIL: the
  task requires existing expectations to stay unchanged, and the behaviour is the same at HEAD. The
  architect should decide last-use versus scope-end for AXS-CAP-003. Scope end needs StorageDead (or
  equivalent scope facts) in pre-elaboration MIR for named locals, which is MIR builder work (T149).
- **N2 (MIR location divergence, existed before T157):** `let mut a = D{x:1}; let v = view(a);
  a = D{x:2}; use_d(v)` gives the same verdict in both compilers but different spans. axiom0 reports
  at the function's `{` and axiomc at `a = ...` (42:5). The MIR text is identical; the location of
  the assignment-drop `drop(_2)` terminator differs between the two MIR builders. At HEAD the outputs
  also differed (axiom0 2× brace, axiomc brace + assignment). For T149/T141 area: axiomc's span is
  the better one.
- **N3 (false negative, same as HEAD):** a non-Copy struct without Drop
  (`struct N { x: i32 }`), moved while viewed and the view used later, is accepted by both compilers
  and by HEAD. This is the engineer's follow-up (pass Copy-ness into the checker).
- **N4:** `tests/std/io/qa_file_roundtrip.ax` races between its legs on fixed temp-file names. Add it
  to the T139 leg-isolation work together with `dir.ax`/`metadata.ax`.
