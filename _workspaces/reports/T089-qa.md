3. **Auto-trait structural solver, built-in rules, negative/unsafe impls, and diagnostics (`compiler/traits/solver.ax`, `compiler/types/typeck.ax`, `compiler/diag/code.ax`, `AXS-CONC-011..018`, `AXS-CONC-050`, `AXS-CONC-052`, `REQ-CONC-219`)**:
   - Verified `Send` and `Share` are tracked and solved independently (`Send != Share`, `AXS-CONC-011`).
   - Verified built-in rules in `type_satisfies_auto_trait`:
     - Primitives (`bool`, `char`, `i8..usize`, `str`, `()`, `!`, `fn(...) -> R`) are `Send + Share`.
     - Raw pointers (`*const T`, `*mut T`) are `!Send` and `!Share` by default.
     - `view<T>` is `Send` iff `T: Share` and `Share` iff `T: Share` (`AXS-CONC-012`).
     - `mut_view<T>` is `Send` iff `T: Send` and unconditionally `!Share` (`AXS-CONC-012`).
   - Verified structural derivation across arrays `[N]T`, slices `[T]`, tuples, structs, enums, and closures (`TY_CLOSURE` capture modes `CAP_MOVE`/`CAP_COPY`, `CAP_VIEW`, `CAP_MUT_VIEW`), with coinductive recursion handling (`AXS-CONC-014`).
   - Verified negative impls (`impl !Send`, `impl !Share`) opt out of structural derivation and propagate to enclosing composites unless overridden by an explicit `unsafe impl` (`AXS-CONC-017`, `AXS-CONC-018`).
   - Verified generic `unsafe impl<T: ...> Send/Share for Wrapper<T>` checks all generic parameter and `where`-clause bounds via `impl_bounds_satisfied` (`AXS-CONC-018`).
   - Verified safe (non-`unsafe`) positive `impl Send` / `impl Share` is rejected by `check_send_share_impl_safety` with `AX_CONC_SAFE_SEND_SHARE_IMPL`, and conflicting positive + negative impls are rejected with `AX_TRAIT_OVERLAP`.
   - Verified `emit_conc_bound_diag` and `find_auto_trait_failure_reason` emit `AX_CONC_NOT_SEND` / `AX_CONC_NOT_SHARE` with a constituent trace note (`AXS-CONC-050`, `AXS-CONC-052`).
