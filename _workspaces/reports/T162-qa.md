   - **Location**: `compiler/types/typeck.ax:6793-6928` (`resolve_deep_ty`) and `compiler/types/typeck.ax:7009` (`finalize_types`).
   - **Expected**: `selfhost/obj_link_driver/axiomc_e2e.test` passes with `known_divergent=0` (matching `axiom0` on `inspect --emit={mir,lir,mi,mc,obj}`).

Because `finalize_types` (`compiler/types/typeck.ax:7017-7042`) already updates `TY_TUPLE` fields and `TY_ARRAY`/`TY_SLICE` `elem` **in-place** in the interner table (matching `bootstrap/axiom0/src/types/typeck.c`), re-interning in `resolve_deep_ty` during `finalize_ptr_typemap` allocates extra `TypeId`s (e.g. `type#73` instead of `type#29` in `tests/run/array/array_basics.ax`) and alters `TY_FN`/closure lowering (`lea rax, [frame#107 + 0]; mov rcx, rax` vs `mov rcx, [frame#107 + 0]` in `tests/run/closure/fn_traits.ax`), causing 26 `REGRESSION` failures in `axiomc_e2e.test`.
   - **Fix**: Keep `resolve_deep_ty` restricted to `TY_VIEW` and `TY_MUT_VIEW` (matching `bootstrap/axiom0/src/types/typeck.c`), remove `finalize_ptr_typemap(mut_view(*cx), mut_view((*thir_ptr).item_types))` from `finalize_types`, and in `finalize_types`'s in-place interner loop (`compiler/types/typeck.ax:7031, 7037`) call `resolve_ty` (or run the in-place `TY_TUPLE`/`TY_ARRAY`/`TY_SLICE` interner loop before `finalize_ptr_typemap`).
