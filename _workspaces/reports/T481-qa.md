### Audit Summary

1. **Specification & Design Compliance (`SPEC-40` `AXS-INT-023`, `REQ-CORE-230`, `REQ-LIR-007`, `REQ-SIMD-264`, `ADR-0042` §3)**:
   - Audited `compiler/lir/builder.ax` (`lower_binop_checked`, `lb_norm_int_const`, `lower_sat_operand`, `lower_saturating_binop`):
     - `MIR_BINOP_SAT_ADD` (`20`), `MIR_BINOP_SAT_SUB` (`21`), and `MIR_BINOP_SAT_MUL` (`22`) are lowered across all ten 8..64-bit integer types (`u8`, `u16`, `u32`, `u64`, `usize`, `i8`, `i16`, `i32`, `i64`, `isize`).
     - Narrow (`< 64`-bit) constant operands are sign/zero-extended via `lb_norm_int_const`, and narrow non-constant operands are cleaned via `LIR_INST_BIT_OR(raw, 0)` at `int_ty_v` before overflow flag evaluation and sign extraction.
     - Unsigned `SatAdd`/`SatMul` clamp to `uN.MAX` on `CF=1`; unsigned `SatSub` clamps to `0` on `CF=1`.
     - Signed `SatAdd`/`SatSub` clamp to `SAR(lhs, 63) ^ iN.MAX` (`iN.MAX` when `lhs >= 0`, `iN.MIN` when `lhs < 0`) on `OF=1`; signed `SatMul` clamps to `SAR(lhs ^ rhs, 63) ^ iN.MAX` on `OF=1`, handling `MIN * -1 -> MAX`, `MIN * MIN -> MAX`, `MAX * -2 -> MIN`, and `0 * MIN -> 0`.
   - Audited `compiler/backend/session.ax` & `tests/selfhost/lir/qa_lir_seam_edge.ax.in`:
     - Unrecognized `MIR_BINOP_*` codes in `lower_binop_checked` and `MIR_RVALUE_OVERFLOWS_BINARY_OP` set `b.has_ice = true`, causing `lir_lower_ext_opt` to return null `LirBody` and `compiler/backend/session.ax` to report `AX_ICE_INTERNAL` (`130`) via `backend_report_ice` instead of silently falling back to `LIR_INST_ADD`.
