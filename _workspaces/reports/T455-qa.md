### Decision on the removed tests (architect, ADR-0044)
The engineer removed `index_trap_u128_alias` (a real soundness bug) rather than ship it, and reported three further defects. Decision: **the suite is complete for the T455 acceptance criteria** (12 types, boundary/overflow/wrapping/saturating/checked/overflowing/bit/endian/From/TryFrom, Pattern D, deep trees, MIR homogeneity, interp-vs-native) - index lowering, unary `~`, array passing and diagnostic 2014 are not in the task scope list.
